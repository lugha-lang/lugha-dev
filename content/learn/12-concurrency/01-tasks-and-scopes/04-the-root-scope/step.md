title: The root scope
---

`main` can also take `root: Scope`, by type like a capability, as on line 8: a scope as long as the program, which the program waits on before it ends.
Line 9 starts `later` in it, and line 10 is `main`'s last line.
Press Run.

`main` returns first, and the program ends only when `later` has finished.
