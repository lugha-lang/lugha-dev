---
title: Sizes in bytes
---

Whole numbers have methods for sizes in bytes: `kb()`, `mb()` and `gb()` multiply by powers of 1024.
Press Run.

Line 2 prints `8192`, eight times 1024, and line 3 prints 64 times 1024 times 1024.
The result keeps the type of the number it's called on, so line 5's is a `u32`, like `pages`.
