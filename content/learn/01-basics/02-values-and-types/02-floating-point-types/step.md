---
title: Floating-point types
---

Numbers with a fraction are floats, and they come in two types, `f32` and `f64`.
Press Run.

A number written with a point, like `2.0` on line 2, is an `f64`.
It prints `2.0`: a float always prints with its point, so you can tell it from a whole number.
Line 3 is ten million billion written out in full, and a float that large prints with an exponent, `1.0e16`.
Line 4 prints a `bool`, which is `true` or `false`.
